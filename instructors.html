Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before hitting the water with us.

Not at all! Our Beginner IKO course is designed for complete newcomers. We start with theory and safety on the beach before ever touching the water. All you need is a willingness to learn and a sense of adventure.

Students must be at least 12 years old to join our lessons. Participants under 18 require written parental consent before their first session. We also offer dedicated junior programs during school holiday periods.

All equipment is included in your lesson price — kites, boards, harnesses, helmets, and wetsuits. We use the latest gear from Duotone, North, and Cabrinha updated every season. If you own your own gear and prefer to use it, just let us know in advance.

Payment is made on-site by cash or card, or via a deposit link sent after your booking is confirmed. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before your lesson receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the deposit.
