Top 5 Beginner Spots for 2026

Alex Mercer May 12, 2026 5 min read

Finding the right spot is crucial when you're just starting your kiteboarding journey. The difference between waist-deep flat water and choppy offshore conditions can mean the difference between loving the sport and giving up on day one.

1. Kite Beach Lagoon

This is our home spot, and for good reason. With an immense area of waist-deep water protected by a sandbar, it provides the ultimate safe environment for practicing body dragging and water starts. The thermal winds blow cross-onshore almost every afternoon.

"The flatter the water, the faster the learning curve."

2. Sandy Point

Located just 20 minutes down the coast, Sandy Point offers consistent trade winds and zero obstacles downwind. It's excellent for those who have mastered the water start and are ready to ride long stretches.
